Survival & Bushcraft FAQs

What are the first survival priorities?

Pause, assess immediate threats, protect yourself from exposure, make a plan and communicate your location if possible. Priorities depend on the environment and situation; avoid risky improvisation.

Can I drink clear water from a stream?

Not safely by appearance alone. Clear water can contain pathogens or chemical contaminants. Use a treatment method suitable for the hazards and follow its instructions.

Water treatment guide →

Is it always legal to make a campfire?

No. Restrictions vary by location, season, fire danger and land manager. Check official local rules before lighting any fire and use designated facilities where required.

Fire safety guide →

Do I need a compass if I have a phone?

A phone is useful but can lose power, signal or accuracy. For unfamiliar or remote routes, learn map-and-compass basics and carry suitable backups.
