How to Pack for Wet Weather

Protect critical layers, organise your pack and keep sleeping gear dry without overpacking.

PRACTICAL FIELD KNOWLEDGE

Protect the essentials

Keeping insulation, spare clothing, electronics and your sleep system dry is more important than making every item waterproof. Use a pack liner or a few durable dry bags, and separate clean dry clothing from wet layers.

Pack by access

Place items needed during the day near the top or in external pockets: rain shell, water, snacks, map and first-aid kit. Keep emergency equipment reachable. Put heavier items close to your back and centred to maintain balance.

Manage wet clothing

Ventilate when conditions allow and avoid sealing damp clothing with dry insulation. Carry a dedicated bag for wet items. Change into dry layers before stopping for a long break in cold conditions.

Use a rain cover wisely

A pack cover helps with showers but may not protect against prolonged rain or water crossings. A liner inside the pack provides a second layer of protection. Check closures and seams before the trip.

Test your system

Practise packing at home and take a short walk in similar conditions. Adjust what you carry based on the forecast, route, season and your ability to dry equipment safely.

Safety note: This guide is general information, not a substitute for local regulations, professional instruction or emergency advice. Adapt decisions to current conditions.

Zaščiti najpomembnejše

Pomembneje kot popolnoma vodoodporna vsaka posamezna stvar je, da ostanejo suhi izolacijska oblačila, rezervna oblačila, elektronika in spalni sistem. Uporabi notranjo vodoodporno vrečo ali nekaj suhih vreč ter loči suha oblačila od mokrih slojev.

Pakiraj glede na dostop

Stvari, ki jih potrebuješ čez dan, shrani na vrh ali v zunanje žepe: nepremočljivo jakno, vodo, prigrizke, zemljevid in prvo pomoč. Težjo opremo namesti blizu hrbta in čim bolj na sredino, da ohraniš ravnotežje.

Ravnanje z mokrimi oblačili

Ko razmere dopuščajo, oblačila prezrači. Vlažnih oblačil ne zapiraj skupaj s suhimi izolacijskimi sloji. Za mokre stvari imej ločeno vrečo. Pred daljšim postankom v mrazu se preobleci v suha oblačila.

Pametna uporaba dežne prevleke

Prevleka pomaga ob plohah, vendar morda ne zadostuje pri dolgotrajnem dežju ali prečkanju vode. Notranja vreča zagotovi dodatno zaščito. Pred izletom preveri zadrge in šive.

Preizkusi sistem

Pakiranje vadi doma in opravi krajši sprehod v podobnih razmerah. Vsebino prilagodi napovedi, poti, sezoni in možnosti varnega sušenja opreme.
