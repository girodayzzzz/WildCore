Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a permit to fish?

In many locations a licence or permit is required. Rules vary by water, species, season and jurisdiction, so check the local fisheries authority before fishing.

What is a good first fishing setup?

Choose a simple rod and reel suited to the water and target species, a small selection of appropriate tackle, line cutters and safe fish-handling tools. Ask a local tackle shop or club for location-specific advice.

How should I handle a fish responsibly?

Keep the fish wet, minimise handling and air exposure, use suitable tackle and follow local rules for retention, size limits and protected species.
