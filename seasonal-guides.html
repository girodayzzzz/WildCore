Seasonal Outdoor Guides

The same trail can feel like a different world in another season. Plan around daylight, temperature, terrain, water and changing conditions.

SPRING

Spring: changing conditions

Expect wet ground, muddy trails, cold mornings and fast weather changes. Check high-water conditions, snow remnants at elevation and seasonal access restrictions. Carry a dry insulating layer and protect electronics and sleep gear from moisture.

Wet-weather packing →
SUMMER

Summer: heat and hydration

Start early, seek shade during the hottest period and plan reliable water access. Adjust effort to heat and humidity, use sun protection and recognise warning signs of heat illness. Thunderstorms can develop quickly; avoid exposed ridges and water when lightning threatens.

Water safety guide →
AUTUMN

Autumn: shorter days

Plan shorter routes and carry reliable lighting as daylight fades earlier. Wet leaves, frost and changing temperatures affect traction and comfort. Keep spare warm layers dry and check local hunting seasons and access restrictions where relevant.

WINTER

Winter: cold and exposure

Choose routes appropriate to winter skills and equipment. Check avalanche forecasts where relevant, wind chill, ice and road access. Carry navigation backups, insulation, food, water and emergency communication. Turn around early if conditions exceed your training.

Seasonal guidance is a planning aid, not a forecast. Check official local weather, hazard and access information immediately before your trip.
