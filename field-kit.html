03 / SURVIVAL FIELD GUIDE

Field skills that matter.

Practical foundations for safer decisions. Educational content, not a substitute for certified training or emergency services.

01 / Navigation & getting lost

Carry a suitable map, compass and charged phone. If disoriented, stop, stay calm, check your last known position and avoid moving deeper into unfamiliar terrain.

02 / Shelter & exposure

Carry layers and rain protection. Keep dry insulation available and choose shelter away from avalanche paths, falling branches, flood channels and exposed ridges.

03 / Water safety

Clear water can still contain harmful microorganisms. Choose a treatment method suited to likely hazards and follow the manufacturer's instructions. Filters, disinfection and boiling have different limitations.

04 / First aid & emergency calls

Carry a first-aid kit and know how to use it. In a serious emergency, contact local emergency services early, follow dispatcher instructions, protect the scene and monitor the person. EU emergency number: 112 where available.

05 / Weather & turnaround

Check forecasts and official warnings for the exact area and elevation. Set a turnaround time. Turn back when visibility, wind, storms, snow, daylight or group condition make the plan unsafe.

06 / Fire, wildlife & Leave No Trace

Follow local fire bans and protected-area rules. Never leave a permitted fire unattended. Observe wildlife from a distance, never feed it and pack out all waste.
