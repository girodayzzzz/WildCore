STARTER PATH

Outdoor fundamentals

A first learning path covering preparation, navigation, shelter, water and responsible decisions.

MODULE 01

Plan before you go

Route research, weather, communication, timing and turnaround decisions.

Open lesson 1: Build a trip plan

Define the route, distance, elevation, expected duration and escape options. Check official access information and current forecasts. Share your route, group details and return time with a trusted person. Set a turnaround time before departure and revise the plan if conditions change.

Open lesson 2: Before departure

Check access, daylight, group capability and communication coverage. Carry a backup plan and agree on what conditions will make you cancel or turn back.

MODULE 02

Navigation essentials

Map symbols, contour lines, compass bearings and position checks.

Open lesson 2: Map and compass

Learn the map legend, scale, grid and contour interval. Match landforms to the terrain around you. Keep the compass level and away from metal or electronics. Navigate in short legs between recognisable features, and stop if the landscape no longer matches the map.

MODULE 03

Shelter and campsite

Choose safer ground, understand exposure and protect your sleep system.

Open lesson 3: Choose a site

Avoid dry streambeds, flood-prone hollows, unstable slopes and dead branches overhead. Check whether overnight stays and fires are permitted. Use durable surfaces and keep waste and washing away from water sources.

Full campsite guide →
MODULE 04

Water and fire awareness

Understand contamination risks, treatment choices and fire restrictions.

Open lesson 4: Water is not automatically safe

Clear water may contain pathogens. Use a treatment method appropriate to the likely hazards and follow the manufacturer's instructions. Boiling, filtration and disinfection have different strengths and limitations. Check local fire bans and never leave a permitted fire unattended.
