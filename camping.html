Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

What should I pack for a first camping trip?

Start with shelter, a suitable sleep system, weather-appropriate clothing, water, food, lighting, navigation and a basic first-aid kit. Adapt the list to the forecast, location and trip length.

How do I choose a campsite?

Use permitted designated sites where required. Avoid dry riverbeds, unstable slopes, dead branches overhead and places exposed to flooding or strong wind.

How can I reduce my impact?

Follow local rules, use established paths and sites, manage food and waste carefully, and leave natural objects and wildlife undisturbed.
