Low-Impact Outdoor Trip Planning

Small choices before and during a trip can reduce damage to habitats, wildlife and shared outdoor spaces.

PRACTICAL FIELD KNOWLEDGE

Research the place

Check access rules, seasonal closures, protected habitats, fire restrictions and waste facilities before travelling. Some areas require permits or limit group size and overnight stays.

Choose durable routes

Stay on established trails and use designated campsites where available. Avoid shortcuts that widen paths or damage fragile vegetation. Follow local guidance in muddy or sensitive terrain.

Food, waste and hygiene

Reduce packaging, carry all rubbish out and store food securely so it does not attract wildlife. Dispose of human waste according to local rules; burying it is not appropriate everywhere.

Observe wildlife respectfully

Keep distance, do not feed animals, avoid nests and dens, and observe quietly. If an animal changes behaviour because of your presence, increase distance.

Share the space

Keep noise low, respect private property and other visitors, and leave natural and cultural objects where they belong.

Safety note: General information only. Follow local regulations and qualified training. / Varnostno opozorilo: Splošne informacije. Upoštevaj lokalne predpise in strokovno usposabljanje.

Razišči območje

Pred odhodom preveri dostop, sezonske zapore, zavarovane habitate, omejitve kurjenja in možnosti za odlaganje odpadkov. Nekatera območja zahtevajo dovolilnice ali omejujejo velikost skupine in prenočevanje.

Izberi odporne poti

Ostani na urejenih poteh in uporabljaj označena mesta za kampiranje. Ne ubiraj bližnjic, ki širijo pot ali poškodujejo občutljivo rastlinje. Na blatnem ali občutljivem terenu upoštevaj lokalna priporočila.

Hrana, odpadki in higiena

Zmanjšaj embalažo, vse odpadke odnesi s seboj in hrano varno shrani, da ne privablja živali. Človeške iztrebke odstranjuj po lokalnih pravilih; zakopavanje ni povsod primerno.

Spoštljivo opazuj živali

Ohranjaj razdaljo, živali ne hrani, izogibaj se gnezdom in brlogom ter opazuj tiho. Če žival zaradi tvoje prisotnosti spremeni vedenje, se oddalji.

Souporaba prostora

Zmanjšaj hrup, spoštuj zasebno lastnino in druge obiskovalce ter naravne in kulturne predmete pusti tam, kamor sodijo.
