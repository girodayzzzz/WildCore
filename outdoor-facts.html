15 Outdoor Facts & Field Notes

Quick reminders for safer, more thoughtful time outside. Save a few, share a few, and always check the rules for your destination.

01 / FIELD NOTE

Check the forecast twice

Review it before leaving and again before exposed sections. Conditions can change quickly.

02 / FIELD NOTE

Tell someone your route

Share your route, expected return and what to do if you are overdue.

03 / FIELD NOTE

Carry a navigation backup

Offline maps, a compass and map skills help when batteries or signal fail.

04 / FIELD NOTE

Layer before you sweat

Vent or remove a layer on climbs; add insulation when you stop.

05 / FIELD NOTE

Keep water separate from fuel

Store drinking water away from fuel, chemicals and contaminated equipment.

06 / FIELD NOTE

Clear water is not always safe

Pathogens and chemicals may be invisible. Treat natural water appropriately.

07 / FIELD NOTE

Know your filter's limits

Check exactly which organisms and contaminants your filter is designed to remove.

08 / FIELD NOTE

Keep a fire small

Small fires are easier to supervise, control and extinguish.

09 / FIELD NOTE

Never leave flames unattended

If you leave or go to sleep, extinguish it completely first.

10 / FIELD NOTE

Pack out every piece of rubbish

Small scraps, food waste and fishing line can harm wildlife.

11 / FIELD NOTE

Stay on durable surfaces

Use established trails and campsites where required to reduce erosion.

12 / FIELD NOTE

Respect quiet hours and other visitors

Keep noise low and give people space to enjoy the outdoors.

13 / FIELD NOTE

Observe wildlife from a distance

Never feed, corner or approach wild animals or their young.

14 / FIELD NOTE

Check local rules before fishing or hunting

Permits, seasons, access and protected areas differ by location.

15 / FIELD NOTE

Leave a plan B

A route change, storm or closed trail should not leave you without options.
