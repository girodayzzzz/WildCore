This notice explains the limited information handled when you visit the current WILDCORE website.

English

2. Information handled

The current website has no visitor accounts, newsletter signup or on-site checkout. Depending on the tools you use, browser local storage may hold language preference, checklists, expedition plans and Academy progress. The Fishing Log stores catch entries and optional photos in IndexedDB on your device. These records are not sent to WILDCORE by the static website; they are not synced between devices and may be lost if you clear site data or change browser/device. Export backups from the relevant tools. The Field Station and maps use third-party services, including OpenStreetMap map tiles and geocoding, public routing services, Open-Meteo weather data, Overpass map data and ARSO map services. When you use these features, your browser connects directly to those providers; they may receive your IP address and the location/search coordinates needed to fulfil the request. GPS access is requested only when you choose the location feature and is handled by your browser. Review the providers' privacy terms for their processing details. The hosting provider and network infrastructure may also process technical request data such as IP address, browser details and server logs to deliver and secure the website.

3. External and affiliate links

External websites operate under their own privacy notices and may use cookies or similar technologies. Some links may be affiliate links; see our Affiliate Disclosure.

4. Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to processing. You can exercise applicable data-protection rights by contacting the operator at info.dayzzzz@gmail.com or by post at the business address above. You can clear browser local storage through your browser settings.

5. Changes

This notice must be reviewed when analytics, advertising, embedded media, forms, accounts, newsletters or e-commerce are introduced.

2. Obdelava podatkov

Trenutna spletna stran nima uporabniških računov, prijave na novice ali zaključka nakupa. Lokalna shramba brskalnika lahko glede na uporabljeno orodje vsebuje jezikovno nastavitev, sezname opreme, načrte odprav in napredek v učilnici. Ribolovni dnevnik shranjuje ulove in morebitne fotografije v IndexedDB na tvoji napravi. Statična stran teh zapisov ne pošilja WILDCORE; podatki se ne sinhronizirajo med napravami in se lahko izgubijo ob izbrisu podatkov strani ali menjavi brskalnika oziroma naprave. V posameznih orodjih izvozi varnostne kopije. Field Station in zemljevidi uporabljajo zunanje storitve, med drugim kartografske podlage OpenStreetMap, geokodiranje, javne storitve za izračun poti, vremenske podatke Open-Meteo, podatke Overpass in kartografske storitve ARSO. Ob uporabi teh funkcij se brskalnik neposredno poveže s ponudniki, ki lahko prejmejo tvoj IP-naslov in lokacijo oziroma koordinate iskanja, potrebne za izvedbo zahteve. Dostop GPS se zahteva samo, ko sam izbereš funkcijo lokacije, dovoljenje pa upravlja brskalnik. Preveri tudi pravila zasebnosti posameznih ponudnikov. Ponudnik gostovanja in omrežna infrastruktura lahko obdelujeta tehnične podatke, kot so IP-naslov, podatki o brskalniku in strežniški dnevniki.
