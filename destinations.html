Featured Slovenian destinations

These are inspiration points, not guarantees of access. Some routes require fitness, equipment, fees or seasonal checks.

01 / VIEWPOINT

Peč viewpoint, Bohinj

An accessible panorama above Lake Bohinj, with views toward the valley and surrounding Julian Alps.

Official destination info ↗
02 / VIEWPOINT

Vogar, Bohinj

A popular mountain viewpoint above the northern shore of Lake Bohinj; a good starting point for longer walks.

Official destination info ↗
03 / WATERFALL

Peričnik Waterfall

An iconic waterfall in the Vrata Valley, known for the path that passes behind its lower cascade when conditions allow.

Official tourism info ↗
04 / WATERFALL

Savica Waterfall

A well-known Bohinj natural attraction at the edge of the Triglav National Park area. Check current visiting arrangements.

Official destination info ↗
05 / ALPINE LANDSCAPE

Velika Planina

A high mountain plateau known for its herdsmen’s settlement, broad views and walking routes. Cable-car operations are seasonal.

Destination info ↗
06 / WATER & GORGE

Mostnica Gorge & Voje Valley

A scenic alpine river gorge and valley near Stara Fužina, with waterfalls and natural features along the route.

Official destination info ↗
07 / NATURE RESERVE

Zelenci Nature Reserve

A distinctive spring and wetland area near Podkoren. Stay on marked paths and respect protected habitats.

Official tourism info ↗
08 / ALPINE VALLEY

Soča Valley

An alpine valley for scenic walks and outdoor activities. River access and water activities may have local rules and safety restrictions.
