How to Choose a Safe Campsite

A practical guide to terrain, weather exposure, water, drainage and low-impact campsite selection.

PRACTICAL FIELD KNOWLEDGE

Check permission first

Before choosing a spot, confirm whether camping is permitted. Protected areas, private land and local rules may restrict overnight stays, fires or access. Use established campsites where required and respect closures.

Read the terrain

Avoid dry streambeds, gullies, floodplains and low depressions where water can collect during a storm. Stay clear of avalanche paths, rockfall zones, unstable banks and slopes with loose soil. Never pitch beneath dead or damaged branches.

Consider wind and weather

Look for natural shelter without placing the tent under hazardous trees. Avoid exposed ridgelines in storms and keep a safe distance from water edges. Check the forecast and understand how wind direction, temperature and rain may change overnight.

Think about water and hygiene

Choose a location that protects water sources from contamination. Keep washing, toilets and waste well away from streams and lakes, following local requirements. Do not assume clear water is safe to drink.

Leave the site better

Use durable surfaces, minimise vegetation damage, pack out all waste and avoid creating new fire rings. If conditions deteriorate, move to a safer permitted location rather than staying for convenience.

Safety note: This guide is general information, not a substitute for local regulations, professional instruction or emergency advice. Adapt decisions to current conditions.

Najprej preveri dovoljenja

Pred izbiro prostora preveri, ali je kampiranje dovoljeno. Zavarovana območja, zasebna zemljišča in lokalni predpisi lahko omejujejo prenočevanje, kurjenje ali dostop. Kjer je zahtevano, uporabljaj urejene kampe in spoštuj zapore.

Preberi teren

Izogibaj se suhim strugam, grapam, poplavnim območjem in kotanjam, kjer se ob nevihti nabira voda. Ne postavljaj šotora na plazovita območja, pod krušljive stene ali na nestabilne brežine. Nikoli ne spi pod suhimi ali poškodovanimi vejami.

Upoštevaj veter in vreme

Izberi naravno zavetje, vendar ne pod nevarnimi drevesi. Ob nevihtah se izogibaj izpostavljenim grebenom in ohrani varno razdaljo od vode. Preveri napoved ter razmisli, kako se lahko veter, temperatura in dež ponoči spremenijo.

Voda in higiena

Izberi prostor, ki ne ogroža vodnih virov. Umivanje, stranišče in odpadke umakni od potokov in jezer ter upoštevaj lokalna pravila. Bistra voda ni nujno pitna.

Za seboj pusti čim manj sledi

Uporabljaj odporne površine, ne poškoduj rastlinja, odnesi vse odpadke in ne ustvarjaj novih kurišč. Če se razmere poslabšajo, se premakni na varnejšo dovoljeno lokacijo.
