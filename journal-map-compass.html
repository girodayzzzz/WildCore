Map and Compass Basics

Learn how to orient a map, understand contour lines, take a bearing and navigate with a backup plan.

PRACTICAL FIELD KNOWLEDGE

Start with the map

Use a topographic map at a useful scale and identify the map legend, scale, grid, north lines and contour interval. Keep the map protected from rain and make sure it covers the full route plus nearby alternatives. Before leaving, mark the start, destination, junctions, water crossings and escape routes.

Understand the terrain

Contour lines join points of equal elevation. Close lines indicate steep terrain; widely spaced lines indicate gentler slopes. V-shaped contours often point uphill along valleys, while ridges form the opposite pattern. Match the shapes on the map to what you can see around you.

Use a compass carefully

Keep the compass level and away from phones, metal objects and vehicle bodies. Align the direction-of-travel arrow with your route, rotate the housing to match the map's north reference, and account for magnetic declination where relevant. Practise in a familiar area before relying on bearings in remote terrain.

Navigate in short legs

Choose identifiable features as intermediate checkpoints. Confirm your position at junctions and terrain changes rather than walking for hours on a single bearing. If the landscape no longer matches the map, stop, assess and retrace to the last known point instead of guessing.

Carry a backup

Tell someone your plan, carry a charged phone and offline map, and bring spare power where appropriate. Electronics complement—not replace—map and compass skills. In poor visibility or dangerous conditions, turn back early.

Safety note: This guide is general information, not a substitute for local regulations, professional instruction or emergency advice. Adapt decisions to current conditions.

Začni z zemljevidom

Uporabi topografski zemljevid primernega merila in preveri legendo, merilo, mrežo, severne črte ter interval plastnic. Zaščiti ga pred dežjem in poskrbi, da pokriva celotno pot ter možne obvoze. Pred odhodom označi začetek, cilj, križišča, prečkanja vode in izhodne poti.

Razumi teren

Plastnice povezujejo točke z enako nadmorsko višino. Zelo skupaj narisane črte pomenijo strm teren, redkeje razporejene pa položnejšega. Oblike dolin in grebenov primerjaj z dejanskim okoljem.

Pravilna uporaba kompasa

Kompas drži vodoravno in stran od telefona, kovinskih predmetov ter vozil. Smerno puščico poravnaj s smerjo poti, upoštevaj sever na zemljevidu in po potrebi magnetno deklinacijo. Vadi na znanem terenu, preden se zaneseš na azimut v odročnem okolju.

Hodi po krajših odsekih

Za vmesne kontrolne točke izberi prepoznavne značilnosti. Položaj preverjaj na križiščih in ob spremembah terena. Če se okolica ne ujema z zemljevidom, se ustavi in vrni do zadnje zanesljivo določene točke.

Rezervni načrt

Nekomu sporoči načrt, vzemi napolnjen telefon in zemljevide brez povezave ter po potrebi dodatno baterijo. Elektronika dopolnjuje, ne nadomešča znanja zemljevida in kompasa. Ob slabi vidljivosti ali nevarnih razmerah se pravočasno obrni.
