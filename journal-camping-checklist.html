Camping & Hiking

The Beginner's Camping Checklist

A practical checklist for planning your first night outdoors, from shelter and sleep to water, navigation and low-impact camping.

Your first camping trip does not need a complicated equipment list. The best starting point is a simple plan that matches the place, forecast, length of stay and the people going with you.

1. Shelter and sleep

Choose a tent or other permitted shelter suitable for the expected conditions. Check that all parts are present before leaving. Add a sleeping bag rated for the likely temperatures and an insulated sleeping mat; the ground can draw heat away even on mild nights.

2. Clothing and weather

Use layers that can be added or removed. Pack a waterproof outer layer, a warm layer and dry spare socks. Check the forecast shortly before departure and understand that mountain, coastal and forest conditions can differ from the nearest town.

3. Water, food and cooking

Carry enough drinking water for the route and planned stay, with a realistic reserve. Bring food that can be stored safely and prepared with the equipment available. Use cooking appliances only where permitted, on a stable surface and away from dry vegetation. Never leave a flame unattended.

4. Navigation and safety

Know the route, estimate the time needed and tell someone your plan. Carry a charged phone, an offline map or suitable navigation backup, a basic first-aid kit, a light and a way to signal for help. Do not rely on mobile coverage being available.

5. Leave the place as you found it

Use designated campsites where required, follow fire restrictions, keep noise down and take all rubbish away. Respect wildlife and other visitors. Before setting off, check access rules, permits and local restrictions for the exact area.

A simple pre-trip checklist

  • Route, weather and local rules checked
  • Shelter, sleeping bag and mat tested
  • Water, food and suitable clothing packed
  • Navigation, light, first aid and phone charged
  • Someone knows your plan and expected return
WILDCORE field note

Conditions and local rules can change. Check current official information, adapt this guidance to your environment and choose the safer option whenever in doubt.
