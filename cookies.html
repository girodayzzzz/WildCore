Cookie Policy

Information about cookies and browser storage used by the current WILDCORE website.

English

Current website

The current website does not intentionally set analytics or advertising cookies. It uses browser local storage for the language preference and, when you use the tools, for checklists, expedition plans and Academy progress. The Fishing Log uses IndexedDB to store your entries and optional photos locally on your device. These are browser storage mechanisms, not cookies. They are not synced between devices and may be removed when you clear site data.

External websites

External websites linked from WILDCORE may use their own cookies and similar technologies. Their policies apply after you leave this website.

Future changes

If analytics, advertising, embedded media or other non-essential technologies are introduced, this policy and the consent mechanism must be reviewed before activation where required by law.

Slovenščina

Trenutna spletna stran

Trenutna spletna stran namenoma ne namešča analitičnih ali oglaševalskih piškotkov. Uporablja lokalno shrambo brskalnika za jezikovno nastavitev, sezname opreme, načrte odprav in napredek v učilnici. Ribolovni dnevnik uporablja IndexedDB za lokalno shranjevanje vnosov in morebitnih fotografij na tvoji napravi. To so mehanizmi shranjevanja brskalnika, ne piškotki. Podatki se ne sinhronizirajo med napravami in se lahko izbrišejo ob čiščenju podatkov strani.

Zunanje spletne strani

Zunanje strani, na katere vodijo povezave WILDCORE, lahko uporabljajo lastne piškotke in podobne tehnologije. Po odhodu s te strani veljajo njihova pravila.

Prihodnje spremembe

Če bodo uvedeni analitika, oglaševanje, vdelani mediji ali druge neobvezne tehnologije, je treba pred aktivacijo, kjer to zahteva zakonodaja, pregledati to politiko in mehanizem za soglasje.
