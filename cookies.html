Cookie Policy

Information about cookies and browser storage used by the current WILDCORE website.

English

Current website

The current website does not intentionally set analytics or advertising cookies. The language selector stores your language preference in browser local storage so the site can remember English or Slovenian on your next visit.

External websites

External websites linked from WILDCORE may use their own cookies and similar technologies. Their policies apply after you leave this website.

Future changes

If analytics, advertising, embedded media or other non-essential technologies are introduced, this policy and the consent mechanism must be reviewed before activation where required by law.

Slovenščina

Trenutna spletna stran

Trenutna spletna stran namenoma ne namešča analitičnih ali oglaševalskih piškotkov. Izbirnik jezika shrani jezikovno nastavitev v lokalno shrambo brskalnika, da si stran ob naslednjem obisku zapomni angleščino ali slovenščino.

Zunanje spletne strani

Zunanje strani, na katere vodijo povezave WILDCORE, lahko uporabljajo lastne piškotke in podobne tehnologije. Po odhodu s te strani veljajo njihova pravila.

Prihodnje spremembe
