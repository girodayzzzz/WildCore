The Field Station.

A practical workspace for your next outdoor day: build a route, check a forecast and keep a record of your adventures.

01 / ROUTE PLANNER

Build a route

Search two places to calculate a road or cycling route preview. The route follows the selected public routing profile, not necessarily a marked hiking trail.

Routing and geocoding are provided by public services (Nominatim / OSRM). Respect their usage policies. Always verify trail access, closures, terrain and current conditions. This is not an offline navigation or emergency service.

02 / WEATHER DESK

Check local conditions

Look up a place for a current conditions snapshot and a short forecast.

Forecast data: Open-Meteo. Mountain weather can differ sharply from valley forecasts; check official mountain bulletins and local warnings before departure.

03 / TRIP LOG

Record an adventure

Create a private trip note on this device. Entries are stored only in this browser and are not uploaded or shared with other visitors.

04 / FIELD READINESS

Pre-departure check

A short decision checklist. Tick each item only after you have actually checked it.

A checklist cannot guarantee safety. Change or cancel plans when conditions, ability or equipment are not suitable.

05 / KEEP LEARNING

Field skills library

Build sound habits before you need them. Start with these practical foundations.

Navigation & lost-person response

Carry a map and compass, know your last confirmed position, stop when uncertain and avoid walking deeper into unknown terrain.
