WILDCORE FIELD GUIDE

Responsible Wildlife Observation

How to observe animals without disturbing them, and what to record in a useful field journal.

PRACTICAL FIELD KNOWLEDGE

Distance is respect

Use binoculars or a camera with a suitable lens instead of approaching. Never surround, chase, touch or feed wild animals. Give extra space to nesting, denning, feeding and resting animals.

Read behaviour

Alarm calls, defensive postures, interrupted feeding or repeated attempts to move away can indicate disturbance. Increase distance and leave calmly.

Protect sensitive locations

Avoid publishing exact locations of rare species, nests or dens. Follow access restrictions and seasonal closures, and keep dogs controlled according to local rules.

Keep useful field notes

Record date, approximate time, habitat, weather, behaviour and identification confidence. Note what you observed rather than guessing. Tracks and signs can be useful when direct identification is uncertain.

Leave no trace

Stay on durable routes, take rubbish away and avoid damaging vegetation for a better view. Good observation does not change an animal's natural behaviour.

Safety note: General information only. Follow local regulations and qualified training. / Varnostno opozorilo: Splošne informacije. Upoštevaj lokalne predpise in strokovno usposabljanje.

Razdalja pomeni spoštovanje

Uporabi daljnogled ali fotoaparat z ustreznim objektivom namesto približevanja. Divjih živali ne obkrožaj, preganjaj, dotikaj se jih ali hrani. Več prostora jim pusti med gnezdenjem, počitkom in hranjenjem.

Opazuj vedenje

Opozorilno oglašanje, obrambna drža, prekinjeno hranjenje ali ponavljajoče umikanje lahko pomenijo vznemirjanje. Povečaj razdaljo in mirno zapusti območje.

Varuj občutljive lokacije

Javno ne objavljaj natančnih lokacij redkih vrst, gnezd ali brlogov. Upoštevaj omejitve dostopa in sezonske zapore, pse pa imej pod nadzorom skladno z lokalnimi pravili.

Vodi terenski dnevnik

Zapiši datum, približen čas, habitat, vreme, vedenje in zanesljivost prepoznave. Zabeleži opažanja, ne ugibanj. Sledi in drugi znaki so lahko koristni, kadar neposredna prepoznava ni zanesljiva.

Ne puščaj sledi

Ostani na odpornih poteh, odnesi odpadke in ne poškoduj rastlinja zaradi boljšega pogleda. Dobro opazovanje ne spremeni naravnega vedenja živali.
