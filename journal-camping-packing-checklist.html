CAMPING & HIKING

Camping Checklist: What to Pack Before You Go

Use this simple framework to organise your gear before leaving. Adapt the list to the campsite, forecast, trip length and local rules.

A good packing routine saves time and helps prevent forgotten essentials. Divide your equipment into clear groups, check it before loading and avoid packing items that do not suit the trip. This guide is a starting point, not a universal list.

1. Shelter and campsite setup

  • Tent, poles, pegs and guylines, checked before departure
  • Groundsheet or footprint where suitable
  • Mallet or suitable peg tool if needed
  • Small rubbish bags and campsite organisation items

2. Sleeping system

  • Sleeping bag suited to expected night temperatures
  • Insulated sleeping mat and repair kit
  • Pillow or compact alternative
  • Dry sleepwear stored separately

3. Clothing and personal items

  • Weather-appropriate base and insulating layers
  • Waterproof jacket and suitable footwear
  • Spare socks and underwear
  • Toiletries, towel, sun and insect protection

4. Food, water and cooking

  • Drinking water and refill plan
  • Meals and snacks suited to the trip
  • Stove, fuel and lighter only where permitted
  • Cookware, utensils and food storage
  • Cleaning supplies and a way to pack out waste

5. Safety and navigation

  • First-aid kit and personal medication
  • Map, offline navigation and charged phone
  • Headlamp and spare batteries
  • Emergency contacts and trip plan shared with someone

6. Before you leave

Confirm your booking or access permission, check local fire and camping rules, review the weather and make sure your vehicle or transport plan is ready. Test unfamiliar equipment at home. Tell someone when you expect to return.

Pack by category, not at random

Use separate bags for sleep, clothing, cooking and safety equipment. Keep items needed first near the top, and store wet or dirty gear separately on the way home. A short final checklist at the door is often the easiest way to catch what is missing.

WILDCORE field note

Conditions, access rules and local regulations can change. Check current official information, adapt this guidance to your environment and choose the safer option whenever in doubt.
