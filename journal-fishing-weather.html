Reading Weather for a Fishing Trip

Understand wind, pressure trends, temperature, water conditions and the importance of a safe exit plan.

PRACTICAL FIELD KNOWLEDGE

Look beyond the forecast icon

Check hourly conditions, wind direction and gusts, precipitation timing, temperature, lightning risk and official warnings. Conditions at a lake, river or coast can differ from the nearest town.

Wind changes the water

Wind can make casting difficult, create waves and make exposed banks or small boats unsafe. Identify sheltered access and avoid the water when conditions exceed your skills or equipment.

Watch water levels

Heavy rain upstream can raise river levels even when skies are clear at your location. Check official hydrological information and avoid flooded banks, fast currents and slippery edges.

Dress for waiting

Fishing can involve long periods with little movement. Use layers, protect hands and feet, carry rain protection and bring dry spare clothing when needed.

Set a cancellation rule

Decide in advance what wind, lightning, flood warning or visibility conditions mean postponing the trip. Tell someone where you will be and when you expect to return.

Safety note: General information only. Follow local regulations and qualified training. / Varnostno opozorilo: Splošne informacije. Upoštevaj lokalne predpise in strokovno usposabljanje.

Ne glej samo ikone napovedi

Preveri urno napoved, smer in sunke vetra, čas padavin, temperaturo, nevarnost neviht in uradna opozorila. Razmere ob jezeru, reki ali morju se lahko razlikujejo od napovedi za najbližje mesto.

Veter spremeni vodo

Veter oteži metanje, ustvarja valove in lahko naredi izpostavljene bregove ali manjše čolne nevarne. Poišči zavetne dostope in se vodi izogni, če razmere presegajo tvoje znanje ali opremo.

Spremljaj vodostaj

Močan dež višje ob toku lahko dvigne gladino reke, tudi če je pri tebi jasno. Preveri uradne hidrološke podatke ter se izogibaj poplavljenim bregovom, hitrim tokovom in spolzkim robovom.

Obleci se za mirovanje

Ribolov pogosto pomeni dolga obdobja z malo gibanja. Uporabi sloje, zaščiti roke in stopala, vzemi zaščito pred dežjem in po potrebi suha rezervna oblačila.

Določi pogoje za odpoved

Vnaprej določi, pri kakšnem vetru, nevihtah, poplavnem opozorilu ali slabi vidljivosti izlet odpoveš. Nekomu povej, kje boš in kdaj se nameravaš vrniti.
