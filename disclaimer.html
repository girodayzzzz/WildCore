Outdoor Information Disclaimer

Important safety and accuracy limitations for WILDCORE outdoor articles and guides.

English

General information

WILDCORE articles are educational starting points, not professional instruction, emergency advice or a substitute for hands-on training.

Conditions and local rules

Weather, terrain, access, fire restrictions, fishing and hunting rules, permits and wildlife risks vary by location and time. Verify current official guidance before acting.

Personal responsibility

Outdoor activities involve risk. Assess your abilities and conditions, use appropriate equipment, tell someone your plan and contact local emergency services when needed. Never rely on a website as your sole source for a safety-critical decision.

Accuracy

We aim to keep information useful and accurate but cannot guarantee every page is complete, current or suitable for your circumstances.

Slovenščina

Splošne informacije

Članki WILDCORE so izhodišče za izobraževanje, ne strokovno usposabljanje, nujna pomoč ali nadomestilo za praktično učenje.

Razmere in lokalna pravila

Vreme, teren, dostop, omejitve kurjenja, ribolovni in lovski predpisi, dovolilnice ter tveganja za stik z živalmi se razlikujejo glede na kraj in čas. Pred ukrepanjem preveri aktualna uradna navodila.

Osebna odgovornost

Dejavnosti v naravi vključujejo tveganja. Oceni svoje sposobnosti in razmere, uporabi primerno opremo, nekomu sporoči načrt poti in po potrebi pokliči lokalne reševalne službe. Pri varnostno kritičnih odločitvah se nikoli ne zanašaj samo na spletno stran.

Točnost

Trudimo se ohranjati uporabne in točne informacije, vendar ne moremo jamčiti, da je vsaka stran popolna, aktualna ali primerna za tvoje okoliščine.
